## Introduction

**_Natarcie Netartu_**, presented at **Galeria Zachęta in Warsaw on 14 June 2003**, marked an important moment for Polish net art: a moment when works born in and for the internet began to enter broader institutional visibility without losing their experimental character.

As the catalog makes clear, this was not simply an exhibition of art placed online. It was an attempt to show a field of practice shaped by the specific logic of the network itself: hyperlinks, browsers, code, distributed authorship, interaction, speed, copying, and circulation. In that sense, net art appeared here not as an extension of older media, but as a medium with its own language, aesthetics, and conditions of reception.

What gives the catalog its lasting value is its closeness to the scene it documents. It preserves not only the works and statements of artists active in the late 1990s and early 2000s, but also the atmosphere of that period, when the internet still seemed like an open territory for artistic invention rather than a finished system. The publication captures a milieu that was diverse, self-aware, and already reflecting critically on its own tools.

Seen today, **_Natarcie Netartu_** remains more than a record of a single exhibition. It is also a concise document of an early moment in Polish network culture, assembled from within that world and attentive to its energy, ambitions, and emerging vocabulary.
