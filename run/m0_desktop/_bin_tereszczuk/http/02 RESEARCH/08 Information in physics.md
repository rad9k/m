# Executive Summary: The Exhaustion of the Bio-Mechanical Model

For over 14 years, our research group—comprising academics and specialists from the **Warsaw University of Technology**, **IBM Poland**, the **Nature Laboratory**, and the **Subtle Energies Laboratory**—pursued an experimental and theoretical program centered on one core conviction: that modern science has approached the limits of the traditional **bio-mechanical model**.

During the last century, physics and biology became highly successful in studying matter and energy. Yet they largely ignored **Information** as an active constituent of reality. In our view, Information is not merely a mathematical description of state, as in Shannon’s framework, but a physical factor capable of shaping the behavior of complex systems. Our long-term aim was to move such phenomena out of the domains labeled “paranormal” or “fringe” and into a space of rational, critical, and eventually reproducible inquiry.

At the same time, we fully acknowledge that the research presented here does **not** satisfy contemporary scientific standards in the strict sense. The central problem is not only controversy of interpretation, but also incomplete repeatability. In our experience, experimental outcomes appeared to depend on the conjunction of two characteristics in the operators conducting them:  
1. the presence of what would today be described as **extrasensory** abilities, and  
2. a **strong, success-oriented intention** directed toward the experiment.  

This means that the observer is not passive, as classical science would prefer, but instead becomes an active part of the experimental system.

# Foundational Research Hypotheses

Our research was anchored in a set of radical but, in our view, experimentally motivated hypotheses that challenge the present scientific status quo:

* **Information as a Physical Foundation:** We propose that Information is a basic element of the Universe, standing alongside matter/energy and space/time. If Information is integrated into fundamental physical law, then phenomena usually classified as “paranormal” may instead be reinterpreted as lawful but insufficiently understood.
* **Qualia and the Limits of Classical Theory:** The nature of information in complex systems goes far beyond what classical information theory can describe. In our framework, information can be *experienced* or *felt* by a subject, which creates a bridge between the physical and the mental.
* **The Measurement Paradigm:** Information can, in principle, be measured, modified, and created within a controlled laboratory environment. This requires a new measurement paradigm in which the measuring apparatus and the observed system are linked through informational resonance.
* **Regulatory Dominance in Complex Systems:** In systems of high internal complexity—such as ecosystems, industrial installations, or living organisms—information acts as the primary regulator of performance, independently of raw flows of energy and matter.
* **Informational Resonance:** Complex informational structures may resonate with subtler levels of reality, facilitating the emergence of higher-order phenomena such as intuition, divination, and consciousness itself.
* **The New Great Unification:** While 20th-century physics sought the unification of the fundamental forces, we propose a broader unification: **Nature and the Aware Mind**.

# Experimental Evidence: From Thermal Processes to Ecosystems

Our hypotheses were not based solely on speculation. Over the years, a number of experimental programs were undertaken to test whether informational intervention could produce measurable physical effects.

## Early Thermal Activation Experiments on Carbon (Wrocław, 2008)

One of the earliest experiments was carried out in **2008 at the Wrocław University of Science and Technology**. The result appeared highly promising.

In this experiment, carbon placed in a thermogravimetric setup was exposed to **light carrying information**. According to the measurements, this intervention significantly reduced the apparent activation energy of the carbon, observed as a substantial reduction in ignition temperature.

Under ordinary conditions, the carbon used in this type of setup ignites at approximately **850–900°C**. In the experimental series, ignition reportedly occurred at approximately **450–500°C**. In our interpretation, this suggested that informational influence may alter the energetic threshold of a material process without changing the material substrate in any conventional chemical sense.

This experiment was repeated several weeks ago, with a **similar result**, which we consider noteworthy even if not yet sufficient for formal scientific validation.

## Informational Influence on the Specific Heat of Water (Wrocław, 2009)

In the summer of **2009**, in the laboratory of **Prof. Marian Szarycz** at the **Wrocław University of Environmental and Life Sciences**, another striking result was obtained.

Water was exposed to **light carrying information**, after which its specific heat was measured. The observed result suggested a reduction of the specific heat of water by **nearly 30%**. If taken at face value, such a result would be extraordinary, as it would imply that informational intervention can measurably alter one of the most basic thermodynamic properties of a substance.

The host professor, reportedly alarmed by the result, declined to formally confirm the experiment. Nevertheless, a printout from the measuring apparatus was preserved in our archive. We present this episode not as definitive proof, but as an example of the type of anomaly that motivated continued research.

## Aquatic Microecosystems and Informational Carriers (The Puchalski Series, 2014–2015)

A major part of our evidence base comes from ecosystem-scale experimentation. In **autumn 2014** and **spring 2015**, **Dr. Wojciech Puchalski** carried out a series of experiments involving the introduction of **infoenergetically modified aluminosilicate**, specifically **halloysite**, into a model ecosystem.

The conclusions were clear at the observational level: the aluminosilicate sample produced **significant changes in the circulation of elements** within the ecosystem. In our interpretation, the introduced material acted not merely as a chemical additive, but as an **informational carrier**, capable of reorganizing interactions among the living and non-living components of the system.

It is important to note that the informational preparation of the sample was highly unconventional. The encoding procedure reportedly involved elements such as **astrological symbols**, **radiesthetic colors**, and **mentalist coefficients**. Any orthodox scientist informed of such preparatory methods would likely reject the experiment outright. Yet the ecological effects observed in the system were substantial enough that we could not ignore them.

### Extended Microcosm Work

A related line of work involved **4-month experiments on complex aquatic microecosystems** (5 L systems), including producers, consumers, and decomposers. In these studies, one portion of the systems was treated with **coded aluminosilicates** functioning as informational carriers.

* **Key findings:** The coded variants exhibited measurable shifts in metabolic efficiency, including increased functional diversity in algal assemblages and a marked increase in oxygen production, in some cases by as much as **5 mg L⁻¹**.
* **Conclusion:** The introduced information appeared to act not on isolated species, but on the **network of interactions** between species, increasing the overall coherence and thermodynamic efficiency of the system.

## Informational Modification in Agriculture (The Zylbertal Series)

Between **2013 and 2015**, we also explored the application of informational modification to agricultural substrates and hydroponic media.

* **Methodology:** Soils and growth media were supplemented with material carriers intended to transmit biologically relevant signals through water present in the substrate and plant tissues.
* **Results:** Plants grown in the modified media showed improved development and increased biomass accumulation relative to controls.
* **Longer-term interpretation:** Six-month observations suggested that informational factors may create a form of ecosystem memory, possibly stored in complex organic compounds such as **hopanoids**, that is, terpenoid structures produced by microorganisms.

# The Challenge of Repeatability: The Operator Effect

We fully recognize that the greatest objection to this body of work is the problem of replication.

The experiments described above do **not** satisfy present-day scientific criteria in a strict sense, because they are not unconditionally repeatable. In our experience, the outcome depends on a specific conjunction of human factors:

1. the operator must possess abilities that would today be described as **extrasensory**, and  
2. the operator must maintain a **strongly focused intention** directed toward experimental success.

When these conditions are met, the experiments tend to succeed repeatedly. When they are absent, the results often disappear.

This leads to a fundamental departure from classical science: the observer is not passive. The observer participates in the formation of the result.

## Stabilizing the Experimental Environment

In an attempt to reduce variability, we proposed preliminary methods for standardizing the operator’s condition:

* **Brainwave Synchronization:** Experimental success appeared more likely when the operator entered a state of neurological coherence.
* **Alpha Rhythm:** Synchronization of dominant brainwave activity within the **Alpha range (8–12 Hz)** seemed to reduce mental noise and facilitate informational coupling.
* **Hemispheric Synchronization:** Balancing left and right hemispheric activity may improve the operator’s stability as part of the measuring system.

These ideas remain provisional, but they represent our effort to move from anecdotal success toward a teachable operational methodology.

# Significance: The Future of Science

Why did we consider this line of inquiry potentially revolutionary?

1. **Experimental Access to Information:** It suggests that information may be treated not only as abstraction, but as an experimentally active factor in physical systems.
2. **A New Medical Horizon:** If life processes are regulated informationally as well as materially and energetically, then future medicine may need to include informational diagnostics and intervention.
3. **A New Ecology:** If ecosystems are structured not only by matter and energy but also by stored and transmitted information, then biodiversity, mineral structure, and biological complexity acquire a deeper functional significance.
4. **A Broader Epistemology:** Science may need to acknowledge that subjectivity is not always noise to be eliminated, but may in some cases be part of the phenomenon itself.

Science, like religion, can become trapped in the defense of inherited dogma. Our work was motivated by the belief that scientific inquiry must remain open to poorly understood phenomena, provided they are approached critically and honestly—even when the available methods remain imperfect.

## Investigations of Information Load in Aquatic Microecosystems

This four-month study (2015) tested the impact of **coded aluminosilicate** on 30 diverse aquatic microecosystems. By comparing coded variants against uncoded controls across different pH levels and leaf types, the research observed how "informational load" alters biological behavior.

### Key Experimental Results:
* **Enhanced Decomposition:** The coded mineral significantly accelerated organic matter breakdown, particularly in **complex, mixed-leaf systems** (up to a 15% increase), while having little effect on simple, single-species setups.
* **Biological Shift:** Coded systems moved away from restrictive cyanobacterial films toward **oxygen-rich green algae and diatoms**, which support a healthier food chain.
* **Metabolic Efficiency:** Coded variants showed substantially higher photosynthetic activity, with dissolved oxygen levels increasing by as much as **5 mg/L** in some treatments.

### The "Complexity" Principle:
The study concludes that informational coding is most effective in **diverse environments**. It suggests that the information does not merely stimulate individual cells but **synchronizes species interactions**, leading to greater metabolic efficiency and ecosystem stability.

> **The Takeaway:** Coded information acts as a "biological synchronizer," proving most powerful in complex systems where it optimizes species interactions and accelerates natural life cycles.

**The Takeaway:** This research challenges the reductionist, DNA-only paradigm of biology, proposing a "Science of Qualities" where the human mind and natural systems are part of a single, informational reality.

## Energetic and Environmental Effects of Vedic Rituals (India Report)

This report details a 2007–2008 field study in India investigating the intersection of **Vedic science** and **environmental ecology**. Using specialized tools like the Life Energy (LE) Meter and magnetometers, the research explores how rituals and sacred sites influence the "subtle" energetic fabric of nature.

### Key Observations & Findings:

* **The Narmada River's Unique Energy:** Due to its rare east-to-west flow (geostrophic effect), the river maintains unusually high energy levels. The water acts as a "purifying" rather than "fertilizing" force, contributing to the creation of natural lingams (energetic stones).
* **Life Energy (LE) Dynamics:** Using a meter based on Wilhelm Reich’s concepts, the study found that **Agnihotra** does not follow a fixed energetic pattern. Instead, its effects are **homeostatic and context-dependent**, influenced by the specific site, the participants, and even the surrounding bird songs.
* **The Somayag Ceremony:** Described as a massive "energy transformer," this ritual involves intense production and uptake of LE. The materials used (e.g., *Soma* stalks, specific wood) exhibit extraordinary energetic properties with potential implications for future material technology.
* **Sacred Geography & Magnetism:** Sacred groves and ancient temples were found to be situated on **geomagnetic anomalies or vortexes**. The use of paramagnetic volcanic soils and construction materials suggests an intentional design to influence human physiology and consciousness.

### Practical Implications:

The study suggests that **Homa Therapy** (fire rituals) acts as a remedy for chaotic magnetic fields and enhances ecosystem health, particularly in nutrient-poor or polluted areas.

> **The Takeaway:** The report advocates for a "New Great Unification" where academic science, parascience, and Vedic spirituality cooperate to create a sustainable, multidimensional understanding of reality.
