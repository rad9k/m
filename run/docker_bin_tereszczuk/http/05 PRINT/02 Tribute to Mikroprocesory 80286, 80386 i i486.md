## About publication

A poetry book published in **Warsaw** at the turn of **1995 and 1996** as part of the **PUPA festival**, a recurring event held at the **Staromiejski Dom Kultury** in Warsaw’s Old Town.

The title of the publication was taken from the name of a book documenting advanced **x86 processor programming techniques**. At the time, it was the only book available in Polish that covered the issues of so-called **protected mode**. Advanced aspects of protected mode, such as access to the full **RAM address space**, management of **hardware multitasking**, and **virtualization** were absolutely essential to the development of the advanced **32-bit operating system** I was working on at the time.

Interestingly, I was never able to buy the book itself - **_Mikroprocesory 80286, 80386 i i486_** and only managed to obtain a **photocopied edition** sold at the computer market on **Grzybowska Street**.

The 'Tribute to _"Mikroprocesory 80286, 80386 i i486"'_ book was printed in an edition of **100 copies**, and, interestingly, the entire run sold out within just a few days.
