# Biography

**Radek Tereszczuk** (born 24 September 1979 in Warsaw). From Ursynów, currently based in Śródmieście. Reality engineer, poet, musician, theorist and pioneer of technology-based art. Inventor, expert and consultant in fields such as computer science, telecommunications, statistics, machine learning, genetics and physics. In his spare time, he develops a research project on a new class of programming languages based on his own discoveries in graph mathematics (_fractal graphs_).

Pioneer of the Polish Internet — among other things, the author of one of the first websites in the field of art ([darta.art.pl](http://darta.art.pl) in 1995). He played his first concert in 1996 and, though not very frequently, continues to perform regularly to this day. Initiator of the _"30 Years of the Polish Techno Scene"_ initiative, through which, with the help of 200 invited contributors, he documented the Polish techno scene in the form of a substantial book.

Professionally active since 1995. [linkedin](https://www.linkedin.com/in/radek-tereszczuk/)

A happy husband and father.
